Hot Tubs & Spas

Garden Leisure Hot Tub

The Garden Leisure line of spas offer everything you are looking for in amenities, beauty, and serenity for your life. Available features include stainless steel jets with the option for backlighting, headrests that are padded, stitched, and UV-treated, and an extra-large footwell. They have superior engineering, featuring a five-point insulation system, energy-efficient filtration, and maintenance-free skirting.

Garden Leisure Swim Spa

A Garden Leisure Swim Spa is perfect for when a full size in-ground or above ground pool is not an option due to property size or other local restrictions.

Available in:

  • 13′ Premium or Party/Exercise
  • 17′ Premium Swim Spa
  • 19′ Dual Swim Spa
  • LED Lighting
  • Stainless Steel Grab Bars and Jets
  • Padded Head Rests
  • Stereo System with available Bluetooth
  • Rowing Kits with resistance bands
  • Water Purification System
  • Digital Topside Controls & More!
